Rule 3: Turn-Based Combat

In DungeonVerse, combat is turn-based, meaning you and your enemies take turns acting in battle. Strategic decisions and the use of the right skills at the right time are key to victory. Every move counts, so plan your actions carefully!

Combat Basics:

  • Attack: Choose your primary offensive action, whether it's a basic melee strike, a ranged attack, or a magical spell. Each character class has its own attack options and damage output.
  • Defend: Sometimes the best way to survive is to defend. Use defensive abilities to reduce incoming damage or avoid status effects like poison or paralysis.
  • Abilities: Every character has unique abilities that can turn the tide of battle. These could include powerful area-of-effect spells, buffs to your stats, debuffs to enemies, healing, or even crowd control tactics like stunning or freezing foes.
  • Items: Use consumables such as health potions, mana potions, or special items to heal, buff, or deal damage to enemies. Items can be a game-changer during tough battles.

Strategic Tips:

  • Use the environment: Some battles may offer environmental factors such as terrain advantages or hazards that can be used to your benefit.
  • Know your enemy: Each enemy type has strengths and weaknesses. For example, some enemies might be weak to fire, while others are resistant to physical attacks. Use this knowledge to plan your attacks accordingly.
  • Take turns carefully: Plan your attacks and defense based on your turn order. Knowing when to attack, heal, or defend can be the difference between victory and defeat.
  • Team synergy: If you're playing with a team, coordinate your moves to create powerful combos. For example, a warrior may use a taunt to keep enemies occupied while a mage deals damage from a distance.

Turn Order:

The order of turns in combat is determined by your character's speed or initiative. Characters with higher speed usually take their turns before slower characters, so it's important to act quickly if you're in a time-sensitive situation. Some abilities may also alter the turn order, allowing you to get additional turns or delay enemy actions.
